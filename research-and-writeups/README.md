## Academic Capstone Research

### 1. Scattered Spider — Master's Capstone Investigation

**Title:** Investigative Report on the Threat Group: Scattered Spider  
**Program:** M.S. Cybersecurity Investigations and Intelligence  
**Institution:** Robert Morris University  
**Completed:** May 7, 2026  
**Length:** Approximately 40 pages  
**Research Type:** Threat Intelligence / Cybercrime Investigation

[View the Scattered Spider Master's Capstone PDF](scattered-spider-masters-capstone.pdf)

#### Overview

This graduate capstone is an intelligence-style investigation of the financially motivated threat actor commonly known as **Scattered Spider**.

The report examines the group's identity-focused intrusion model, social-engineering tradecraft, enterprise targeting, attribution challenges, financial motivations, law-enforcement activity, and long-term threat outlook.

Key areas of analysis include:

- Scattered Spider attribution and associated aliases
- Relationship to the broader cybercriminal ecosystem known as "The Com"
- Analysis of overlapping or adjacent actors and operations
- Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH)
- Social engineering and help-desk impersonation
- MFA manipulation and identity compromise
- Cloud, SaaS, and enterprise infrastructure targeting
- MITRE ATT&CK technique mapping
- MGM Resorts and Caesars Entertainment case studies
- Transport for London analysis
- Snowflake-related activity
- Marks & Spencer and DragonForce-linked activity
- Cryptocurrency and extortion models
- Law-enforcement disruption and prosecution
- Defensive-control alignment
- Intelligence gaps
- Future threat outlook

The investigation emphasizes a central finding: Scattered Spider's effectiveness is driven less by novel malware and more by its ability to exploit **identity systems, organizational trust, support processes, and legitimate administrative access**.

### 2. Cyber Threat Analysis in the Financial Sector — Undergraduate Capstone

**Title:** Cyber Threat Analysis in the Financial Sector  
**Program:** B.S. Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics  
**Institution:** Robert Morris University  
**Completed:** November 23, 2025  
**Length:** 13 pages  
**Research Type:** Financial-Sector Cybersecurity / Threat Analysis

[View the Financial Sector Undergraduate Capstone PDF](financial-sector-undergraduate-capstone.pdf)

#### Overview

This undergraduate capstone analyzes major cybersecurity threats affecting financial institutions and evaluates how technical controls, security policy, employee training, and layered defenses can reduce organizational risk.

The report focuses on three major threat categories:

- SQL Injection
- Phishing
- Ransomware

The analysis includes:

- Financial-sector attack surface and risk
- Confidentiality and integrity impacts
- Customer trust and reputational damage
- SQL Injection prevention and secure coding
- Web Application Firewall concepts
- Phishing simulations and email security
- MFA and domain-authentication controls
- Ransomware detection and containment
- Endpoint Detection and Response
- Network segmentation
- Offline and isolated backups
- Vulnerability scanning
- Incident-response planning
- Regulatory and security-framework considerations

The report also evaluates real-world incidents involving financial institutions and other major organizations to demonstrate how technical vulnerabilities, human weaknesses, and third-party risk can lead to substantial operational and financial consequences.
