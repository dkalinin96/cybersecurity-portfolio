## Security Operations & Incident Response

### 01 - Vulnerability Assessment and Digital Forensics

**Week 6, Days 1-3 - Cybersecurity for Professionals Bootcamp**  
**Focus: Vulnerability Management | Threat Intelligence | Digital Forensics**

A multi-day security assessment involving vulnerability scan triage, manual validation of findings, CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities research, and risk-based remediation prioritization. The lab then transitioned into a digital forensic investigation involving volatile evidence collection, timeline reconstruction, IOC analysis, and root-cause identification.

[View Vulnerability Assessment & Digital Forensics Lab →](./bootcamp-labs/week6-days1-3-vulnerability-assessment-digital-forensics.pdf)

### 02 - Threat Hunting and Detection Engineering

**Week 6, Days 4-5 - Cybersecurity for Professionals Bootcamp**  
**Focus: Threat Hunting | MITRE ATT&CK | Detection Engineering**

An extension of the forensic investigation focused on determining the full scope of compromise across multiple systems. The lab included network telemetry analysis, lateral-movement investigation, Pyramid of Pain classification, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, Sigma detection development, and containment, eradication, and recovery planning.

[View Threat Hunting & Detection Engineering Lab →](./bootcamp-labs/week6-days4-5-threat-hunting-detection-engineering.pdf)

---

### 03 - SIEM Triage and Incident Response

**Week 7, Days 1-2 - Cybersecurity for Professionals Bootcamp**  
**Focus: SOC Operations | SIEM Triage | Business Email Compromise | Incident Response**

A Security Operations Center exercise involving the triage of twenty alerts across authentication, impossible travel, business email compromise, and suspicious wire-transfer activity. The investigation required runbook-based decisions, alert correlation, escalation documentation, incident scoping, containment planning, and response to a simulated business email compromise involving financial fraud.
